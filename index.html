Where Victoria's rubbish goes

Every year Victoria's 79 councils collect about 2.35 million tonnes of rubbish, recycling and food scraps from the kerb. Half of it never reaches landfill — but which half depends a great deal on where you live.

How well do councils recycle

Councils are required to report how much of what they collect is kept out of landfill. Across the state the median council diverts about half. The map shows no clean line between the city and the country.
